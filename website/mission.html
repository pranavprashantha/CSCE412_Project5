Mission

Why we run

To make running a shared, welcoming habit for everyone in our town, at every pace and every age.

  • 01

    Everyone finishes together

    No one is dropped. We regroup, loop back and wait.

  • 02

    Free to join

    Running should not cost anything. Our runs never will.

  • 03

    Leave it better

    We pick up litter on our routes and respect the trails we use.

  • 04

    Show up, not show off

    Consistency matters more than speed. Coming back next week is the goal.
