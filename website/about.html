Who We Are

A small club with an open door

Fieldline started as three friends running the same loop every Tuesday. Today it is a group of runners of every age and pace who meet a few times a week to share the miles.

How we run

Every run has a set route and a sweeper at the back, so nobody gets left behind. Faster runners loop back, slower runners set the pace, and everyone finishes together.

There is no membership fee and no sign-up needed for a run. Just show up a few minutes early and say hello.
